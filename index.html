Autonomous Vehicle Trust Analysis

Project ROUTE data science challenge

Analysed 2,500 synthetic autonomous-vehicle journeys to decide when the ride can be trusted, from data validation through a predictive model to a conditional trust decision.

The decision: conditional trust, with human oversight in bad weather and no unsupervised rides in snow. The problem rate rises from 15.7% in clear weather to 47.7% in snow, and sensor alerts act as an early warning: about 0% problems at 0-1 alerts against 85% at 4 or more.

Loan Default Risk Model

Lloyds Banking Group Step Up Virtual Career Challenge

Profiled customers who repaid versus defaulted across 18,324 historical loans, then built a calibrated model that scores new customers into risk bands.

PythonPandasscikit-learnLogistic Regression
Show details

Loan term is the strongest driver of default: 60-month loans default at 31.9% against 17.1% for 36-month loans. Affordability (repayments relative to income) matters more than income itself. The model's scores read as genuine default probabilities, and the high-risk fifth of customers defaults at 39.8% against 12.7% for the low-risk half.

21.3% of the 18,324 loans defaulted0.695 test ROC-AUC (95% CI 0.673 to 0.717)2.6x default odds for 60-month vs 36-month loans

A gradient boosting challenger did no better (AUC 0.690), so the simpler, explainable logistic regression was kept. The data is US data supplied for the challenge, and the write-up notes the model would need UK retraining and a fairness review before any real lending use.
