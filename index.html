Kenya AI Carbon Audit

A BigQuery pipeline unifying four data sources — AI workload logs, GPU hardware specs, data center PUE, and Kenya's national grid generation mix — into a single model that calculates the real energy and carbon cost of AI training and inference, run locally instead of assumed against a generic global average.

322.56 kWh total energy, sample workload 91.4% renewable grid mix (geothermal, hydro, wind) ~80% lower emissions vs. coal-heavy grids 10–20% overhead added by data center cooling (PUE)

The finding: Kenya isn't a compromise location for AI compute, it's a genuine advantage. Running the same workload here instead of on a fossil-heavy grid cuts emissions by roughly 80% before touching a single line of code — though cooling and facility overhead still add a real, measurable tax on top of raw compute.
